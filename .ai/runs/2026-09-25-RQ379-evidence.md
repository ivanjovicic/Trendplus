## Interpreted outcome and owner

Supplier Sales decision payloads must be validated at the runtime fetch
boundary. The schema owns the fields consumed by page arithmetic, sorting,
recommendation display, cost-quality labels and export/trust projections. It
must fail closed for malformed data while accepting valid zero, nullable and
signed margin evidence.

## Runtime behavior

- Added `supplierSalesStatsResponseSchema` covering response metadata,
  supplier rows, footwear breakdowns, weighted RQ378 benchmark fields,
  cost-source coverage, totals, recommendation status/reasons and seasons.
- `getSupplierSalesStats` now passes the schema to `fetchAnalyticsJson`, so
  validation failures use the existing safe analytics error path.
- Signed revenue and margin contribution remain accepted, including margin
  percentages below `-100` when the signed denominator permits them.
- Malformed recommendation percentages, missing response sections, invalid
  dates and empty cost-source basis fail validation rather than becoming a
  normal or zero-valued state.
