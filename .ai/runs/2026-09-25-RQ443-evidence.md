## Root cause

`buildSupplierSalesDisplayProjection` summed `previousPeriodRevenue` over the visible rows. `GET /api/analytics/supplier-sales-stats` builds rows only from current-period sale lines (`AllEndpoints.cs:1430+`) while the previous period is summed over all lines (`:1281-1317`), so a supplier with previous-period sales and no current sales was missing from the base and „Ukupan PoP trend“ was biased upward.

The page uses `response_totals` (backend `totals.previousPeriodRevenue`) for „Svi dobavljači“, `visible_rows` for a focused supplier and `unavailable` (N/A) for „Poznati dobavljači“.
